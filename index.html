Royal Roiers · Side product

A profit and loss bot for crypto traders

  1. You pasted the transaction ID of the trade you wanted to show into the bot.
  2. The bot generated an image with your profile and your trade.
  3. It showed your profit or loss, or the potential result in real time if you had not sold.

Sold to Discord communities, whose members shared their trades on Twitter and so marketed the groups. We were early; the market soon filled with competitors offering the same service. Estimated sales: SEK ~110k.
